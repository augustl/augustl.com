"The year of Linux on the desktop".

Nobody runs "Linux". They run Ubuntu, RedHat, Omarchy.

Nobody runs Mach. They run macOS!

Nobody runs NT. They run Windows 11!

When is the year of NT on the desktop?

Is Android a Linux distribution? People call Android an OS. Why? Why is Android an OS and Ubuntu a Linux distribution? Think about it. Really think about it!

Is Android an OS because it is a better platform? Does a Linux distribution become an OS when it becomes popular? Is it an OS when people make apps for it, that only runs on it and nothing else?

Is SteamOS a Linux distribution? Or is it a mobile and desktop hybrid gaming OS?

Linus Torvalds, of course, [agrees with me](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qHGTs1NSB1s). It doesn't even make sense to say that you have a "Linux version" of your product. You have an Ubuntu version, a RedHat version. You use QT or GTK. You support Xorg or Wayland. This is modular mayhem, and not a desktop OS.

Maybe this year, maybe next, Omarchy will be mainstream. There will be a booming industry of Omarchy software. And alas, Linux will be running on desktops everywhere. And it will not be the year or Linux on the desktop.
